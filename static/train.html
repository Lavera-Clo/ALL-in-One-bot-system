Train from your website

Paste your website's address. The bot reads the home page and up to 7 of its most useful pages (prices, menu, delivery, contact…) and learns them like a document. Use Read website again on it later when your website changes.

Automatic test

Asks the bot the questions from your own documents' FAQs, plus checks every bot must pass (admitting what it doesn't know, passing discounts and risky questions to a person). Each reply is checked for the right facts, made-up numbers and the customer's language. Run it before going live and after changing documents. Nothing is sent to WhatsApp.
