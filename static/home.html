How it works

From a PDF to a working assistant in minutes

No forms full of settings. Give it what you already have, and it does the rest.

Create your account

Your name, your business name, an email. You get your own private workspace straight away.

Upload what you have

Price lists, menus, brochures, even scanned pages. Example chats teach it how you like to talk.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need any technical skills?

No. You upload documents and answer a few questions in plain language. The only technical step is connecting WhatsApp, and the setup page walks you through it step by step.

What do I need to connect WhatsApp?

A number on the WhatsApp Business Platform, set up through your own Meta (Facebook) business account. You can build and test your assistant fully before you have one.

Could it tell a customer the wrong price?

It is built not to. Every price and number in a reply is checked against your own documents before it is sent; if it can't confirm a figure, it says it will check with your team instead.

What happens when it doesn't know the answer?

It tells the customer it will check, and the question appears in your list of questions to answer. Answer it once and the assistant uses your answer from then on.

How long does setting up take?

Creating your account takes a minute. Each document usually trains in one to three minutes, after which the assistant tests itself and shows you a score.

Can I change what it says?

Yes. You can correct anything it learned from a document, change its name, tone and hand-over rules, and teach it new answers whenever you like.
